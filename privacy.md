# Privacy Policy for Ain't Listening

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Author / Copyright:** switch-consulting ([https://switch-consulting.de/](https://switch-consulting.de/))

## Overview
"Ain't Listening" ("we", "our", or "us") is an offline-first Android utility designed to transcribe shared voice messages locally on your device. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection & Processing
* **Local Processing:** All audio transcription and text formatting (using Vosk, Whisper, ONNX Runtime, and Deep Java Library) occur **100% locally on your device**. 
* **No Data Collection:** We do **not** collect, store, upload, or transmit any audio files, transcripts, personal information, or analytics to our servers or any third parties.

## Network Usage
* The app requests internet access solely for the purpose of letting you download optional offline speech recognition models (~45MB–75MB) from official model repositories (such as alphacephei.com and HuggingFace). Once downloaded, the app functions entirely offline.
